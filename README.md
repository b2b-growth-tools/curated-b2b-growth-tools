# B2B Sales, Prospecting & Revenue Tools

A neutral, maintained directory of software for B2B prospecting, LinkedIn workflows, CRM data, enrichment, outreach, SEO research, and revenue operations.

This is an editorial list, not a ranking, endorsement, affiliate page, or paid-placement directory. Products are grouped by their primary use case. A product may appear only once unless it has genuinely different products.

## How to use this list

Use the categories to build a shortlist, then compare current pricing, supported regions, integrations, data sources, privacy terms, and platform policies on each product's own website. Descriptions are intentionally short and factual; they are not product reviews.

## Selection and maintenance

- Include products that have a working public website and a clear fit for one of the categories.
- Prefer first-party product pages for descriptions; do not copy marketing claims, rankings, testimonials, or competitor comparisons.
- Keep descriptions to one factual sentence and link to the official product page.
- Do not add products because they paid, exchanged links, supplied a backlink, or requested favorable wording.
- Do not claim that any product is “best,” “safest,” “compliant,” “accurate,” or “official” unless the statement is independently documented and necessary for the reader.
- Review entries at least quarterly. Update changed URLs, product scope, and discontinued tools; record meaningful changes in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Keep the list alphabetical within each category.

## Contributing

1. Open an issue before proposing a large category or structural change.
2. Submit one product per pull request when possible.
3. Include the official product URL, proposed category, and a neutral one-sentence description.
4. Explain why the product belongs in this directory and identify the public source used for its description.
5. Disclose any affiliation with the product. Affiliation does not guarantee inclusion.
6. Maintainers may reject duplicate, inactive, misleading, thin, or promotional submissions.
